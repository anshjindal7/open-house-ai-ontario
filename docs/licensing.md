# Licensing of Open House's sources (checked Oct 7, 2026)

| Source | Commercial use | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Residential Tenancies Act, 2006 | Allowed without permission or charge (Ontario "legal materials") | Reproduce accurately; credit "© King's Printer for Ontario, 2026"; state it isn't an official version. Done in the app. |
| LTB decisions (LTB Order Catalogue) | Allowed (Open Government Licence – Ontario) | Credit exactly: "Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Ontario." The licence **does not cover personal information**, so decisions must stay redacted. Done in the app. |
| Standard Form of Lease (form 2229E) text | **Likely needs a licence** | Ontario: "The materials may only be reproduced for commercial purposes under a licence from the King's Printer, with the exception of Government of Ontario legal materials." The form isn't a statute or decision. |
| LTB Interpretation Guidelines and brochures (Tribunals Ontario) | **Likely needs a licence** | Same rule as above. |

Sources: https://www.ontario.ca/page/copyright-information ·
https://www.ontario.ca/page/open-government-licence-ontario

Contact for a commercial licence: Senior Copyright Advisor, Publications Ontario,
Copyright@ontario.ca, 416-326-5153.

## Until a licence is in place

For a public commercial launch, the safe options are (a) get the licence, or (b) turn off the
standard-lease and LTB-guidance sources (the app still works with the RTA and LTB decisions, but
loses the plain-language explanations and several playbook branches).
