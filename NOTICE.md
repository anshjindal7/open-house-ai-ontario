# Sources and credits

Open House quotes official Ontario sources word for word. The text in `corpus/` (and the quotes
inside `train/data/` and `train/interpret/`) comes from these sources, unchanged apart from being
split into sections, and stays under its owners' terms. It is not covered by the licence of this
repository's code.

contact the Landlord and Tenant Board (1-888-332-3234) or a community legal clinic.
