RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made between Maple Grove Properties LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned ("Tenant") for the premises at 214 Willow Street, Unit 3B.

1. TERM. The lease begins September 1 and ends August 31 of the following year. This Lease shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless Tenant gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the current term.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is $1,850, due on the 1st of each month. Landlord may increase rent upon thirty (30) days written notice at any time during the term.

3. LATE FEES. Rent received after 11:59 p.m. on the 1st is late. Tenant shall pay a late fee of $150 plus $25 per day until rent is paid in full.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850. A $400 administrative fee is non-refundable. At move-out, Landlord will deduct the cost of professional carpet cleaning and full interior repainting regardless of condition.

5. UTILITIES. Tenant pays electricity, gas, internet, and a monthly $65 amenity and technology fee. Landlord pays water and trash.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant is responsible for all repairs to the premises, including plumbing, heating, and appliances, regardless of cause. Tenant shall keep the premises clean and sanitary.

7. LANDLORD ACCESS. Landlord and its agents may enter the premises at any time for inspection, repairs, or showing the unit, with or without notice to Tenant.

8. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant forfeits the entire security deposit and remains liable for all rent through the end of the term, plus an early termination fee equal to two months' rent.

9. GUESTS AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not sublet or assign this Lease. Guests may not stay more than three (3) nights in any thirty-day period without written consent.

10. PETS. One cat is permitted with a $300 pet deposit. No other animals.

11. INDEMNIFICATION. Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from any and all claims, injuries, or damages occurring on the premises, including those arising from Landlord's own negligence.

12. WAIVER. Tenant waives the right to a trial by jury in any action arising from this Lease and waives any statutory notice to quit prior to eviction proceedings.

13. ATTORNEY FEES. In any dispute, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney fees and collection costs.

14. QUIET ENJOYMENT. Tenant may peacefully enjoy the premises so long as Tenant complies with this Lease.

15. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease is the entire agreement between the parties and may only be modified in writing signed by both parties.
